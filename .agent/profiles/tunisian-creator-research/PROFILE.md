# Tunisian Creator Research Profile

## Objective
Build a traceable directory targeting 100 distinct Tunisian content creators using Google Search, YouTube, and other accessible public sources. The number 100 is a target, never a reason to weaken verification.

## Scope
Define creator/entity types and inclusion criteria first. Distinguish a Tunisian creator from a non-Tunisian creator who merely covers Tunisia. Keep language, subject matter, audience, nationality, and residence as separate attributes unless evidence explicitly connects them.

## Workflow
1. Scope and inclusion criteria.
2. Multilingual discovery and query expansion.
3. Candidate deduplication and identity resolution.
4. Channel/profile cross-link verification.
5. Claim-linked evidence ledger and record status.
6. Time-stamped metrics and sampling.
7. Content taxonomy and selected-claim fact checking.
8. Evidence-backed collaboration graph.
9. Bounded public-comment analysis where justified.
10. Consolidation, quality gates, export validation, and release decision.

## Verification threshold
A VERIFIED record requires resolved identity, direct evidence for the relevant Tunisia connection, direct evidence linking the entity to its primary channel/profile, claim-specific source URLs, and a dated verification record. Unresolved contradictions that undermine inclusion block verification. A YouTube channel is not mandatory if the declared scope includes creators on other platforms; record its absence rather than inventing one.

## Required statuses
Record: VERIFIED, PARTIALLY_VERIFIED, UNVERIFIED, EXCLUDED.
Claim: SUPPORTED, MOSTLY_SUPPORTED, PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED, CONTRADICTED, MISLEADING_CONTEXT, UNVERIFIED, NOT_ASSESSABLE.
Release: READY_FOR_PUBLICATION, PARTIALLY_READY, BLOCKED.

## Metrics and ranking
Store metric values with observation dates. Separate reach, activity, and recent performance. Do not infer audience geography, demographics, trustworthiness, or persuasion from aggregate metrics. Publish ranking only with a reproducible formula and comparable data; otherwise provide category lists.

## Coverage and outputs
Track category, language, platform, and region only where supported by evidence. Coverage matrices identify search gaps but do not justify weak records. Deliver a verified directory, separate candidate appendix, exclusion/unverified ledger, source register, metric snapshot, coverage matrix, quality report, change log, and continuation state. Export CSV/JSON when supported.

## Privacy
Use public, relevant information. Do not infer private attributes, sensitive traits, psychological states, political/religious beliefs, or intent from superficial signals. Allegations require specific evidence and careful wording.
