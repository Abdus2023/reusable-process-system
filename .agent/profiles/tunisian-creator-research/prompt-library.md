# Prompt Library — Tunisian Creator Research

Use in sequence for a new project. For continuation, resume from saved state and execute only unfinished work. Prompts are instructions, not evidence that work occurred.

## 01 — Master scope and setup
Using available Google Search, YouTube, and public sources, build a directory targeting 100 distinct Tunisian content creators. Define inclusion/exclusion criteria, entity types, categories, languages, regional coverage, evidence requirements, stable IDs, and output schema. Search in Tunisian Arabic/Darija, Arabic script, French, English, and useful transliterations. Work in batches of 20. Log discovery query, source URL, observation date, and next action. Never invent records or metrics. End with counts, gaps, and continuation state.

## 02 — Continue without resetting
Continue from saved state. Preserve stable IDs, completed phases, sources, decisions, and unresolved issues. Do not repeat completed work or restart numbering. Identify and execute the exact next batch/gap; update cumulative counts and source log. If prior artifacts are inaccessible, disclose that and establish a recovery ledger.

## 03 — Discovery expansion and gap closure
Inspect category, language, region, and platform coverage. Identify under-covered dimensions and run targeted searches. Treat results as leads, not proof. Return up to 20 genuinely new candidates with query provenance, URLs, duplicate checks, and verification priorities. Never add weak candidates solely to reach 100.

## 04 — Identity and cross-platform verification
Resolve canonical entity, aliases, channel URL/ID, linked website, and public profiles. Prefer direct creator-owned/platform evidence and independent corroboration. Document evidence for identity, channel association, and Tunisia connection separately. Check rebrands, impersonators, fan channels, and name collisions. Preserve contradictions and assign explicit status.

## 05 — Metrics and comparison
Capture visible subscribers/followers, views, upload activity, and a declared sample of recent items. Record value, platform, URL, observation date, and direct/third-party/unavailable method. Separate reach, activity, and recent performance. For any ranking, publish formula, weights, date, missing-data policy, and limitations. Otherwise use category lists.

## 06 — Content and editorial classification
Define a repeatable sample window and inspect available titles, descriptions, transcripts, or videos; state what was actually inspected. Classify topics/formats with a documented taxonomy. Distinguish facts, opinions, satire, promotions, and allegations. Extract checkable claims for separate fact-checking. Report sample size and limitations.

## 07 — Collaboration mapping
Identify evidenced co-created videos, guest appearances, interviews, explicit promotions, or event appearances. Record source, date, type, and evidence for every edge. Distinguish direct collaboration from mere mention or shared topic. Do not infer friendship, endorsement, audience overlap, or private relationships. Produce node registry, edge list, and limitations.

## 08 — Claim-level credibility audit
Select checkable public claims and record exact claim, source item, date, and context. Seek primary evidence and counterevidence. Assign SUPPORTED, MOSTLY_SUPPORTED, PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED, CONTRADICTED, MISLEADING_CONTEXT, UNVERIFIED, or NOT_ASSESSABLE. Explain reasoning, cite sources, record corrections, and do not infer intent. Do not convert individual claim results into a blanket judgment of a person.

## 09 — Public audience/comment analysis
Define bounded comment sample, sorting mode, date, and exclusions. Use a transparent codebook for praise, criticism, questions, disagreement, spam, and off-topic responses. Report counts and denominators; account for platform sorting/moderation bias. Do not infer audience-wide beliefs, demographics, representativeness, or persuasion from a convenience sample.

## 10 — Integration, quality assurance, release
Audit IDs, duplicates, channel links, Tunisia evidence, metric dates, claim-source mapping, contradictions, and export consistency. Reconcile status counts. Keep non-verified candidates separate from the verified list. Run the directory validator. Mark gates PASS, FAIL, or NOT_VERIFIABLE; choose READY_FOR_PUBLICATION, PARTIALLY_READY, or BLOCKED. Fewer than 100 is acceptable if fewer meet the evidence threshold. End with counts, unresolved issues, continuity check, and next action.
