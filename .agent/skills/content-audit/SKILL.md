# Content Audit

## Procedure
1. Define the sample: date window, number of recent uploads, sampling rule, and exclusion criteria.
2. Inspect accessible titles, descriptions, transcripts, or video content as available. Label the inspection method; metadata alone is not full video review.
3. Classify themes and formats using a documented taxonomy. Allow multiple categories and an uncertainty label.
4. Distinguish creator-stated facts, opinions, commentary, satire, promotion, and reported allegations.
5. For factual claims selected for checking, create claim-level entries and route them to `credibility-audit`.
6. Record language, topical scope, sample size, date range, and missing items.
7. Avoid generalizing from a small or algorithmically biased sample to the creator's entire output.
8. Separate descriptive analysis from normative judgments.

## Output
Sample inventory, taxonomy, content profile, notable factual claims for verification, and sampling limitations.

## Failure conditions
Do not state that a video was watched if only its title or transcript was inspected. Do not treat a content category as a claim about the creator's private beliefs or intent.
