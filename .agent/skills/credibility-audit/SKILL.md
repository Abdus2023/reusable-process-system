# Credibility Audit

## Procedure
1. Quote or precisely paraphrase one checkable claim, with source item and timestamp/location when available.
2. Separate factual claims from opinions, predictions, jokes, satire, and value judgments.
3. Find primary evidence and credible independent sources; seek disconfirming evidence as well as confirming evidence.
4. Assess date, context, definitions, uncertainty, and whether the evidence addresses the exact claim.
5. Assign one claim status: SUPPORTED, MOSTLY_SUPPORTED, PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED, CONTRADICTED, MISLEADING_CONTEXT, UNVERIFIED, NOT_ASSESSABLE.
6. Cite the evidence immediately with the conclusion. Explain limitations and alternative interpretations.
7. Record corrections, retractions, and material updates with dates.
8. Keep claim-level findings separate from a creator's overall identity or worth. Do not infer intent from an inaccurate statement without independent evidence.

## Output
Claim ledger, source-quality notes, counterevidence, verdict, confidence rationale, and open questions.

## Failure conditions
Do not label an entire creator truthful or untruthful based on a handful of claims. Do not repeat allegations as facts.
