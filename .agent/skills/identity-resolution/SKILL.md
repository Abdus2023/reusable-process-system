# Identity Resolution

## Procedure
1. Establish the canonical entity and entity type: individual, group, channel brand, or media organization.
2. Collect candidate identifiers: display name, handle, channel URL/ID, linked website, and public cross-platform profiles.
3. Prefer direct first-party evidence such as channel About pages, creator-owned websites, verified cross-links, or explicit self-identification.
4. Use independent corroboration where available; repeated copies of one source are not independent corroboration.
5. Assess the Tunisia connection separately from language, subject matter, audience, residence, and nationality. Record exactly what is supported and avoid stronger claims.
6. Compare aliases, rebrands, duplicate channels, fan channels, impersonators, and unrelated people with similar names.
7. Record evidence for each relationship: creator ↔ channel, creator ↔ profile, creator ↔ Tunisia connection.
8. Preserve conflicts and confidence limits. Do not infer identity from a shared name or avatar alone.
9. Assign VERIFIED, PARTIALLY_VERIFIED, UNVERIFIED, or EXCLUDED according to the evidence threshold.

## Output
Canonical entity record, alias/link graph, claim-to-source mapping, conflicts, status, and unresolved questions.

## Stop conditions
Do not merge entities when evidence is ambiguous. Do not label a channel official without evidence linking it to the creator. Use public, relevant information only.
